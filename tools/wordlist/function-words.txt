# Words to keep OUT of the list regardless of how distinct they sound.
#
# A spoken address has to survive being embedded in a sentence: "it is have not
# know" is not parseable, and a listener writing down four words cannot tell
# where the address starts. Content words -- things you can picture -- do not
# have that problem. There is no offline part-of-speech tagger in this build, so
# this is hand-written and deliberately over-broad: the pool is large enough to
# afford it.
#
